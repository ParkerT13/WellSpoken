# Voice reference clip provenance

These clips are the reference audio Chatterbox-Turbo clones its two voices
from. Both are short excerpts from public-domain LibriVox recordings
(LibriVox releases all of its recordings into the public domain), so they're
safe to bundle and ship with WellSpoken:

- `chatterbox_female_ref.wav` - Elizabeth Klett reading the opening of
  *Pride and Prejudice* (Jane Austen), LibriVox.
  Source: https://archive.org/details/prideandprejudice_1005_librivox
- `chatterbox_male_ref.wav` - David Clarke reading the opening of
  *The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes* ("A Scandal in Bohemia", Arthur Conan
  Doyle), LibriVox.
  Source: https://archive.org/details/adventuressherlockholmes_v4_1501_librivox

Each clip is a ~12s excerpt trimmed past the standard LibriVox intro
announcement, verified by transcription to contain clean solo narration with
no background noise or overlapping speech.
